**Protocol 5: save logins, generate passwords, cards and identities**: `save` creates a login or updates one on the server, and the browser sees it at once without a sync; `generate` makes a password or a passphrase, also while the vault is locked; `search` lists cards (brand and last 4 digits) and identities (name or email) besides logins, every row says its kind, and `fill` gives the fields of a card or an identity. A refused save is the error `save_failed`. Protocol 4 browsers are refused.
